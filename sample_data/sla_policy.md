# SLA and Escalation Policy

Critical production outages, enterprise customer incidents, payment disputes, legal threats, account lockouts affecting paid customers, and unresolved high-severity technical issues should be escalated to a human lead.

Initial response targets:
- Critical outage or enterprise workspace down: 1 hour.
- Billing issue or account lockout: 4 business hours.
- Refund or return request: 1 business day.
- General product question: 2 business days.

Escalate when churn risk is high, the customer mentions cancellation, competitor switching, legal action, chargeback, public complaint, data exposure, payment data, or repeated failed resolution.
