# Market Gap Notes — SupportOps AI

## Existing product category

Current support AI platforms already provide AI agents, copilots, omnichannel automation, helpdesk integrations, workflow builders, knowledge-base automation, and analytics.

Examples include Zendesk, Intercom Fin, Freshdesk Freddy AI, and Gorgias.

## Gap for this project

The public demo targets teams that want support intelligence before adopting or migrating into a full helpdesk platform.

### Gap 1: Upload-first bulk support analysis

Many platforms work best after a team has migrated tickets, channels, and workflows into that platform. SupportOps AI can analyze exported CSVs and uploaded docs locally.

### Gap 2: Evidence-grounded replies

The app shows source snippets from policy/FAQ/docs so support drafts are reviewable instead of generic.

### Gap 3: Refund and escalation decision support

The app combines ticket text, policy evidence, order history, safety flags, sentiment, and churn risk into one review table.

### Gap 4: Knowledge-base gap detection

The app identifies categories where evidence is weak or missing and suggests new help articles/macros.

### Gap 5: Human-review-first design

The workflow prepares recommendations and exports. It does not auto-send replies or approve refunds.

### Gap 6: Support QA scoring

Each draft is scored for evidence coverage, empathy, clarity, and review readiness.

### Gap 7: Audit package export

Every decision includes evidence, risk scores, and reply drafts in an exportable JSON package.
